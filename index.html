02 / Featured deep dive

Durex: a 360° digital
growth blueprint.

One connected customer journey, not five disconnected channels. This is an illustrative strategic extension of my documented three-variant launch experience—designed to demonstrate how I would build and measure a complete digital programme.

01DiscoverCompliant awareness, PR, contextual reach
02ConsiderSEO, education, product content
03ConvertRetail PDPs, e-commerce, conversion
04RetainOpt-in CRM and lifecycle journeys
05OptimiseGA4, dashboards, tests and learning

Strategy & audience

Start with adult shopper needs, search intent, category barriers and product differentiation. Build channel-appropriate messages that comply with platform policies and age restrictions.

Awareness & acquisition

Plan permitted contextual placements, PR and creative concepts. Track qualified reach, branded search and content engagement, avoiding sensitive personal targeting.

SEO & content

Map search intent to product FAQs, educational content and retailer PDPs; audit metadata, mobile usability and product information.

Conversion & e-commerce

Improve retailer product-page quality, discoverability, availability and purchase-path clarity. Track PDP views, add-to-cart, conversion and attributable sales where data exists.

CRM & lifecycle

Design consent-first welcome, education and post-purchase journeys, with segmentation, suppression logic and frequency caps.

Measurement & testing

Build a GA4 event plan, UTM naming rules, dashboard KPIs, weekly test backlog and cohort reviews. Prioritise incrementality over vanity metrics.

Illustrative 90-day roadmap

Weeks 1–2: insight, audit, tracking and compliance. Weeks 3–4: content, retailer pages and CRM journey design. Weeks 5–8: phased activation and reporting. Weeks 9–12: experimentation and optimisation.

Digital stack — example architecture

GA4 + Tag Manager + Search Console for web analytics; HubSpot/Salesforce for consented CRM; Excel/Power BI/SQL for reporting; paid-media managers only where the relevant platform permits the category. These are capability demonstrations, not claims that every platform was used in the historical launch.

Experience supported by CV: launch of three variants, cross-functional coordination, SEO/CRM/e-commerce workstreams, and a reported 2.8 percentage-point volume-share gain (pending confirmation of measurement/attribution). Digital mechanics above are a proposed framework, not fabricated campaign results.
